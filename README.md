Tripwise is an iOS app designed to simplify destination choices through a recommendation system based on personal user preferences.
Our goal is to make the decision-making process easier and more mindful by turning travel tastes, needs, and constraints into concrete, tailored suggestions. 
The app allows users to specify preferences such as budget, climate, travel period, desired activities, and experience type, matching them against the characteristics of various destinations. 
A rating system prioritizes the most compatible locations and presents them through an intuitive, immediate interface. 
In doing so, Tripwise reduces research time and helps users effortlessly find the destination that best suits their needs.
